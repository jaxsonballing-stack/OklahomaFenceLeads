Local notes

Oklahoma City fence basics.

COST

What it runs

Cedar privacy fencing across OKC typically runs $22–$42 per linear foot installed — the wide range reflects how much the city spans, from tight urban lots to larger suburban-style yards on the outer edges.

PERMITS

Do you need one

Oklahoma City requires a permit for most fence construction, and specific rules can vary by ward and zoning. Older urban neighborhoods sometimes have tighter setback rules than newer additions on the city's edges.

COVERAGE

Which side of town

OKC is large enough that no single contractor covers the whole city yet. Right now we have crews reliably covering northwest OKC and south OKC — if you're elsewhere in the metro, submit a request anyway and we'll do our best to match you.
