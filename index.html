Frequently asked

Does it cost anything to request quotes?

No. Requesting quotes through Oklahoma Fence Leads is free for homeowners. Contractors pay us, not you.

How many contractors will contact me?

Typically two to three, matched to your city and project type — not a flood of calls.

Do I need a permit for a fence in the OKC metro?

Most cities require a permit for fences over a certain height, and rules vary by city and HOA. We'll note city-specific permit info on each city page as we build them out.
