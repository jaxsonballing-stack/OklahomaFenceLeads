Norman fence basics.

COST

What it runs

Fencing in Norman typically runs $23–$39 per linear foot installed for wood privacy. Chain link tends to run cheaper per foot and is common for rental properties near campus.

PERMITS

Do you need one

The City of Norman requires a permit for most fence construction. Older platted neighborhoods near campus sometimes have narrower easements than newer additions, so it's worth confirming your property line before your contractor sets posts.

TIMING

When to book

Turnover season around student housing (late spring and late summer) keeps crews busy on rental properties — booking a few weeks ahead helps if your timeline lines up with that.
